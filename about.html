About

The Studio

An idea-led creative studio in Athens. The idea leads, the medium follows.

We build worlds, not deliverables.

HiCircus Studio is an idea-led creative studio. We work in 3D, video, motion and concept — whatever the idea demands — and break boundaries to craft images that shouldn’t exist.

Three questions before every project: who is this for, what obvious version did we reject, and what world does this create?

Our Approach

How we work

  1. Listen

    We start with your drawings, references and goals, and agree on the views and moments that matter most.

  2. Compose

    Camera, light and materials are set up like a photo shoot, so every image tells a clear story.

  3. Refine

    Drafts are shared early and refined together until every detail feels right.

  4. Deliver

    Final stills, films, VR experiences and brand assets, prepared for print, web and sales suites.
