Who we are

This website belongs to HiCircus Studio, a studio for architecture, product, motion and AI visualization. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean HiCircus Studio.

What this website collects

Only what you choose to send us. When you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, company (if you add it), the service you are interested in and your message. The form sends these details by email to our studio; they are not stored on the website.

The site has no accounts or newsletter sign-ups, and it does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or any other tracking technology.

All fonts, images and videos are served from our own server, so visiting the site does not send information to third-party services such as font or video providers.

Server logs

Like almost every website, the server that hosts this site may automatically record basic technical information when a page is requested: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, and your browser and device type.

These logs are used only to keep the site secure and working, and are kept for a limited time by our hosting provider.

When you contact us

We use the details you send only to reply and to discuss your project, and we keep your messages for as long as we need them for that conversation and any project that follows. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing.

Links to other websites

Our social profiles on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube and TikTok are run by those platforms, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, object to or restrict its use, and receive a copy of it.

You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

Contact

For any question about this policy, please get in touch through our contact page.
