How we work

We take on projects in one of two ways. Per Project: a single, self-contained deliverable with a scope, timeline and price agreed in writing before we start. Credit Bank: a prepaid balance of studio hours you draw on across as many deliverables as your pipeline needs, over the package's validity period. See Pricing for how each one runs in practice.

Payment terms

Per Project work is invoiced 50% to begin and 50% on final delivery. Credit Bank packages are invoiced 100% upfront; your credits activate once that invoice is paid. All payments are by bank transfer to the account stated on the relevant invoice.

If a Per Project payment is more than 5 days overdue, we may pause work until it's settled. Beyond 15 days overdue, late-payment interest and administrative charges may apply to the outstanding amount.

Revisions & scope changes

Per Project work includes one consolidated round of revisions, gathered as a single set of notes and applied to draft-resolution renders. Credit Bank packages include a set number of revision hours that scales with the package size, detailed on the Pricing page.

Requests beyond that — a second round, changes to geometry or camera angles, new deliverables, or anything outside the original brief — are treated as new work. We'll always estimate and agree the cost or credit total with you in writing before starting it.

Credit Bank validity & balances

Each Credit Bank package is valid for a fixed period from activation, shown on the Pricing page. Credits not used within that period lapse automatically and are not refunded or paid out — unless you purchase a new, larger package before the current one expires, in which case any unused credits carry over into the new balance.

If a project in progress needs more credits than you have available, we'll let you know before your balance runs out. Work pauses until a top-up covers the shortfall, so you never receive a surprise invoice mid-project.

Cancellation policy

Deposits and prepaid Credit Bank balances are non-refundable. If you cancel a Per Project engagement after work has started, we may invoice for the work already completed, in addition to the deposit. If an agreed start date passes without the project actually starting, we'll hold the offer for one further week before it lapses and a new proposal is needed.

Deliverables & usage rights

Once a deliverable is paid in full, you receive a non-exclusive, ongoing licence to use the final files (e.g. JPEG, MP4, PDF) for the business purposes we've agreed. Source files — 3D models, scenes, lighting setups and other work-in-progress material — remain the exclusive property of HiCircus Studio and are not part of the delivery unless separately agreed in writing, at an additional cost.

We keep backups of project files for 30 days after final delivery and recommend you archive your own copies of anything you need to keep. We also keep the right to feature finished work in our own portfolio, website, social channels and award submissions, unless you and we have agreed in writing to keep a project confidential.
